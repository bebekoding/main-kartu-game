# Main Kartu

## Register

product

## Users

Casual Indonesian players using phones and desktop browsers, playing with
friends or bots. Players can also watch an ongoing Remi match.

## Product Purpose

Play Truf, Remi, Cangkul and Tebak Kartu in shared rooms. Keep seats, turns,
scores, ownership and reconnect behavior consistent across connected players.

## Brand Personality

Playful, welcoming, tavern-inspired. Use concise conversational Indonesian.
The established forest and gold identity is defined in ../SKILL.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard redesigns, unnecessary decorative motion, crowded
mobile controls, unexplained score columns and technical implementation copy.

## Design Principles

- Keep the next action clear on a small screen.
- Preserve the current match and require consent for shared decisions.
- Explain waiting, disconnection and recovery states with a way to leave.
- Keep spectator controls available above gameplay overlays.
- Treat saved state and server permission checks as one workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled controls, readable contrast, keyboard focus and text alongside
status colors. New controls should have comfortable touch targets and avoid
unnecessary motion. No additional user-specific accessibility needs are known.
